# Robust physics-informed DRL for semi-active MR-damper control — manuscript and reproduction package

- `manuscript.docx` — the paper (generated by `code/build_paper.py`; tables and in-text numbers are read from `results/`).
- `references.md` — the 73 cited references with links, in order of citation.
- `figures/` — figures used in the paper (`fig_hysteresis.png` is produced but not used).
- `results/` — `tables.json` (all statistics), `baselines.json` (baseline tuning grids, incl. `co_dc`), `validation_dyke.json`,
  `integration_check.json`, `kf_tuning.json`, `codc_diag.json` (prediction-quality diagnostic of the delay-compensated
  controller), and one `.npz` per (scenario set, controller) with per-scenario indices. `tables_3seeds.json` is the
  statistics file of the previous version (seeds 0–2 only), kept for reference.
- `runs/` — 30 trained policies, all with 400 iterations = 4.1 million decisions (`*_s{seed}.pt` = best validation
  checkpoint), training logs (`*.json`, `logs/`): `V_pi_dr` (proposed) and `V_pi_nom` (no domain randomisation) with
  seeds 0–9, `V_raw_dr` (no observer) and `V_pi_sym` (symmetric critic) with seeds 0–4.
- `runs_b100/`, `results/b100/`, `results/tables_b100.json` — policies and results of the earlier 100-iteration budget
  (seeds 0–2 only), kept for the budget comparison in Section 6.8 of the paper.
- `data/` — recorded accelerograms (El Centro from vibrationdata.com; ten records shipped with gmspy 0.1.3).
- `refs/` — Crossref / arXiv metadata used to build the reference list.

## Which numbers come from which runs
- Every PI-DRL number in the tables and figures of Sections 6.2–6.7 and 6.9 is from the 400-iteration (4.1 M-decision)
  policies; "PI-DRL" is the average over the ten seeds of `V_pi_dr`.
- The 100-iteration (1.0 M-decision) results appear only in Section 6.8 (budget table), as the dotted curve of the training
  figure, and in two sentences that refer to that section. That comparison uses seeds 0–2 at both budgets.
- Non-learning baselines (P-OFF, P-ON, P-BEST, P-BEST2, AS, CO-NOM, CO-ROB, CO-DC) do not depend on the training budget.

## Reproduce (Python 3, numpy, scipy, numba, torch (CPU), matplotlib, python-docx); run inside `code/`
1. `python validate_dyke.py` — verification against Dyke et al. (1996), Table 2.
2. `python tune_kf.py` — observer tuning.
3. `python tune_baselines.py`, then `python tune_ext.py`
   (clipped-optimal grid extension), then `python tune_extra.py` (P-BEST, P-BEST2, AS), then `python tune_codc.py`
   (delay-compensated clipped-optimal controller CO-DC: control weight x design delay, same set as CO-ROB).
4. `bash run_queue.sh 400` — the 30 PPO runs listed in `queue.txt` (400 iterations, about 21 min each on one core,
   two at a time, about 5.5 h in total). Finished runs are skipped and interrupted runs resume from their last validation point.
5. `python evaluate.py` — all controllers on all scenario sets (about 2 h on one core; only finished runs are evaluated,
   existing result files are skipped; `--only <name prefixes>` restricts the controllers).
6. `python check_integration.py`, `python diag_codc.py`, `python analyze.py`, `python fig_schematic.py`,
   `python build_paper.py`. `analyze.py` uses every seed that has finished training and has been evaluated; the prose of
   the manuscript is assembled from the resulting statistics (see the `outcomes:` line printed by `build_paper.py`).

Files: `simcore.py` (structure, MR damper, integrator, ground motions), `env.py` (environment, observer, baselines incl.
`ClippedOptimalDC`), `ppo.py` (training), `evaluate.py`, `analyze.py`, `paperlib.py` + `build_paper.py` (manuscript),
`test_codc.py` (development test of CO-DC on the nominal plant), `eval_loop.sh` (evaluates runs as they finish).
